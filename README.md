# bagtag-dubai-marketing-analytics

**INTRODUCTION**
This project presents a comprehensive marketing analytics case study for **BagTag Dubai**, a UAE-based eco-friendly packaging and shopping bag supplier.
The objective of the project was to analyse the company's Instagram marketing performance alongside its sales data to identify customer behaviour, content effectiveness, sales trends, and business opportunities. The analysis was carried out using **Tableau** for interactive dashboards and **Microsoft Excel** for data preparation and exploration.
The final outcome includes four business dashboards supported by a detailed analytical report containing observations, insights, and practical recommendations for improving both marketing performance and sales growth.

**TOOLS & SKILL**
- **Tableau** – Dashboard design and data visualisation
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning and preparation
- **Marketing Analytics** – Performance analysis and business insights
- **Data Visualisation** – KPI dashboards and trend analysis
- **Business Analysis** – Customer, product, and sales performance evaluation

**Project Objectives**
The project was designed to answer the following business questions:
- Which Instagram content formats generate the highest visibility?
- How effectively does Instagram convert viewers into profile visits and link clicks?
- Who is the brand's primary target audience?
- Which customers contribute the highest sales revenue?
- Which product categories generate the greatest sales?
- How have sales changed over time?
- What practical recommendations can improve both marketing performance and business growth?
